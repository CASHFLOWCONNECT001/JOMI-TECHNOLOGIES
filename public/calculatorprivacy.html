Privacy Policy for TST Calculator

Last Updated: November 13, 2026

TechSwiftTrix Agency ("we," "our," or "us") operates the TST Calculator mobile application (the "App").

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App. We have built the TST Calculator to be a 100% offline, privacy-first utility.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.
TST Calculator is designed to function entirely on your device. We do not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal details (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to use the App.

2. Device Permissions

The App does not request or require access to sensitive device features. We do not access your Camera, Microphone, Location (GPS), Contacts, or Personal Files (outside of the App's private sandbox).

3. Local Data Storage

To provide you with a better user experience, the App saves your calculation history and user preferences (such as dark/light mode) locally on your device using secure local storage.

  • This data never leaves your device.
  • We do not have access to your calculation history.
  • You can delete this data at any time by clearing the app's cache in your phone's settings or using the "Clear History" button within the App.

4. Third-Party Services and APIs

TST Calculator does not include any third-party advertising networks, analytics trackers, or tracking SDKs.

However, to provide specific features, the App may securely connect to the following public APIs via the internet:

  • ExchangeRate-API: To fetch live currency exchange rates. (No personal data is transmitted).
  • Nager.Date API: To fetch public holidays for the Date Calculator. (No personal data is transmitted).

These services only receive standard, anonymous technical requests and do not track you across other apps or websites.

5. Children’s Privacy

TST Calculator does not collect any personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because the App does not collect any information, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
